# 🌐 ISO/IEC 27001

[[Google Cybersecurity|← Back to Course Hub]] · [[2026-10-15|Related day: 2026-10-15]]

## 📌 What it is

**ISO/IEC 27001** is an international standard published by the **International Organization for Standardization (ISO)** and the **International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)**.

- It enables organizations of **all sectors and sizes** to manage the security of assets such as:
    - financial information
    - intellectual property
    - employee data
    - information entrusted to third parties

> [!INFO] Why it matters
> Where [[NIST Cybersecurity Framework|NIST CSF]] and [[NIST SP 800-53]] are US-centric, **ISO/IEC 27001** is the **international** benchmark for information-security management.
