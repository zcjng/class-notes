# 📄 NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-53

[[Google Cybersecurity|← Back to Course Hub]] · [[2026-10-15|Related day: 2026-10-15]]

## 📌 What it is

**NIST SP 800-53** provides a **unified framework** for protecting the security of information systems within the **U.S. federal government**.

- Its security controls help maintain the **[[CIA Triad|Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) triad]]** for government systems.
- It is a **control catalogue** — a source of specific, documented controls — as opposed to the higher-level, function-oriented [[NIST Cybersecurity Framework|NIST CSF]].

> [!TIP] CSF vs. 800-53 in one line
> **CSF** = *what outcomes to pursue* (Identify, Protect, Detect…). **SP 800-53** = *the concrete controls* to implement.
