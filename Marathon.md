# 📅 OS Quiz Prep: Ultimate 8-Hour Master Timeline

## 🌅 Phase 1: The First Deep Focus Block (Theory & Mechanics)
* **13:00 – 14:30 (90 mins) | Deep Study Session 1**
	* [ ] Put phone completely out of sight.
	* [ ] Focus on the hardest theory items: **Memory Allocation algorithms** (First-Fit, Best-Fit, Worst-Fit) and **Cache Buffers** (LRU policies).
	* [ ] *Strategy:* Visually sketch how memory blocks are assigned to make the concepts concrete.
* **14:30 – 14:45 (15 mins) | The First Wall-Stare Reset**
	* [ ] Sit back and face a blank wall or empty space. Let your mind drift.
	* [ ] ⚠️ **Strict Rule:** Zero screens, zero inputs, no notifications. Lower your dopamine baseline.
* **14:45 – 16:00 (75 mins) | Deep Study Session 2**
	* [ ] Tackle **File Descriptors** (indexes, stdin/stdout/stderr mechanics).
	* [ ] Cover **Virtualization** and structural differences (Process vs. Batch vs. VMs).
	* [ ] Finish filling out the core worksheet.

---

## 🥪 Phase 2: The Recharge Break (1 Hour)
* **16:00 – 16:30 (30 mins) | Screen-Free Dinner**
	* [ ] Eat a clean, protein-focused meal to prevent a post-meal insulin crash.
	* [ ] ⚠️ **Strict Rule:** Absolutely no YouTube, anime, or social media while eating.
* **16:30 – 16:50 (20 mins) | The Strategic Micro-Nap / NSDR**
	* [ ] Set an alarm for exactly 20 minutes. Close your eyes in a dark room.
	* [ ] *Alternative:* If you can't sleep, play a 15-minute Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR) audio track to clear adenosine.
* **16:50 – 17:00 (10 mins) | Wake Up & Transition**
	* [ ] Stand up, stretch, and reset your desk environment.

---

## 🧠 Phase 3: The Endurance Sprint Block (Application & Practice)
* **17:00 – 17:05 (5 mins) | The Adrenaline Jumpstart**
	* [ ] Do 2 minutes of intense physical movement (jumping jacks/air squats) to blast away post-nap grogginess.
* **17:00 – 19:00 (2 hours) | GitHub Workspace Lab Implementation**
	* [ ] Dive into the **GitHub workspace lab**. 
	* [ ] *Strategy:* Watch how the terminal interacts with memory and file systems to bridge the gap between theory and code.
* **19:00 – 19:15 (15 mins) | The Second Wall-Stare Reset**
	* [ ] Face the wall for 15 minutes of pure boredom. Let the brain clear out the coding fatigue.
* **19:15 – 21:00 (1 hour 45 mins) | Active Recall & Drill Session**
	* [ ] 🚀 *Environment Shift:* If possible, move to a different room or a different table.
	* [ ] Hide your worksheet answers and force yourself to explain the OS mechanisms out loud.
	* [ ] Re-run any tricky sections of the GitHub lab until you can explain the output.

---

## 🏋️‍♂️ Phase 4: The Gym, Sauna, & Nightly Prep Routine
* **21:00 – 21:10 (10 mins) | Travel to the Gym**
	* [ ] Pack gym clothes, toothbrush, toothpaste, and shower gear.
* **21:10 – 22:10 (60 mins) | The Workout**
	* [ ] Complete a moderate workout to relieve stress. 
	* [ ] ⚠️ **Strict Rule:** No pre-workout or late-night caffeine.
* **22:10 – 22:20 (10 mins) | The Sauna**
	* [ ] Sit in the sauna for 10 minutes to sweat out the muscle tension and lower stress hormones.
* **22:20 – 22:45 (25 mins) | Gym Bath & Hygiene**
	* [ ] Take your bath/shower at the gym.
	* [ ] **Brush your teeth right there** at the gym sink while your body cools down.
* **22:45 – 23:00 (15 mins) | Travel Home & Final Prep**
	* [ ] Go straight to the kitchen.
	* [ ] Mix your **overnight oats** for tomorrow morning and put them in the fridge.

---

## 💤 Phase 5: The Hard Stop & Sleep Window
* **23:00 | Lights Out**
	* [ ] Phone goes outside the bedroom or completely out of reach.
	* [ ] Get into bed. Your falling core temperature from the sauna will accelerate deep sleep.
	* [ ] *Neurological Process:* The brain consolidates OS architecture from short-term memory to long-term memory.
* **07:00 – 08:00 | Wake Up**
	* [ ] Wake up naturally. Grab your pre-made overnight oats from the fridge.
* **10:00 | The OS Quiz**
	* [ ] Walk in fully rested and crush it.
