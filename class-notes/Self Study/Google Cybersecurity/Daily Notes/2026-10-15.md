## 📝 Raw Notes

> [!TIP] Frameworks vs. controls vs. posture
> - **Security frameworks** = *guidelines for building plans* to mitigate risk and threats to data and privacy.
> - **Security controls** = *safeguards* designed to reduce specific security risks; used *with* frameworks to establish a strong security posture.
> - **Security posture** = an organization's ability to manage its defense of critical assets and data and react to change.

### Logs (M3)
- A **log** = record of events in an organization's systems and networks, including outbound requests to the internet.
- **Firewall log** = attempted/established connections for incoming traffic · **Network log** = devices entering/leaving the network + device/service connections · **Server log** = events for services (websites, email, file shares) like login/password/username requests.

### SIEM (M3)
- Collects & analyzes log data to monitor critical activities; creates dashboards; **metrics** = response time, availability, failure rate.
- **Alerts** reduce the data an analyst must review. **SOAR** = orchestration/automation/response to security events.
- **TLDR:** SIEMs are evolving toward automation + cloud. IoT growth enlarges the attack surface.
- Hosting: **self-hosted** (physical control), **cloud-hosted** (vendor-managed, easy setup), **hybrid** (both), **cloud-native** (built for cloud: availability, flexibility, scalability).
- Tools: **Splunk Enterprise** (self-hosted), **Splunk Cloud**, **Chronicle** (Google, cloud-native) · **Suricata** (open-source network threat detection).
- **Open-source** = free, collaborative, customizable · **Proprietary** = owned code, paid usage (Splunk, Google SecOps).

### Playbooks (M4)
- IR phases: **Preparation → Detection & Analysis → Containment → Eradication & Recovery → Post-incident activity** (reflection/lessons learned).
- **Coordination** = sharing & reporting info *throughout* the entire process (reporting, standards-based sharing, compliance, unified response).
- Incident & vulnerability response playbooks follow the organization's **business continuity plan**.
- **Chain of custody** = documenting evidence possession & control; report every move of evidence.
- **Protecting & preserving evidence** = handle fragile/volatile data per the **order of volatility**; improper handling ruins evidence — always work on **copies**. → [[Playbooks & Incident Response]]
