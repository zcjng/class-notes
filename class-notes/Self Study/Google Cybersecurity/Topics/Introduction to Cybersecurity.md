# 🛡️ Introduction to Cybersecurity

[[Google Cybersecurity|← Back to Course Hub]] · [[2026-10-14|Related day: 2026-10-14]]

## 📌 Definition

**Cybersecurity** ensures the **[[CIA Triad|CIA triad]]** by protecting systems, networks, and data from outside threats.

> [!EXAMPLE] Confidentiality in practice
> Wanting users to choose *complex passwords* improves **confidentiality** by making it harder for a [[Threat Actors|threat actor (TA)]] to break in.

A **threat actor (TA)** is any person or group who presents a security risk.

---

## 🎯 Why Organizations Need Security

1. **Protect against external and internal threats**
    - **External threat** — comes from *outside* the organization, trying to gain access.
    - **Internal threat** — current or former employees, external vendors, or trusted partners.
        - *Accidental:* clicking a phishing email or a compromised link.
        - *Intentional:* abusing systems or engaging in unauthorized access.
2. **Meet regulatory compliance** — follow laws or guidelines by implementing specific security standards; helps avoid fines and audits.
3. **Maintain business productivity.**
4. **Reduce expenses** — recover from data loss and limit downtime.
5. **Maintain brand trust** — if data is lost, the business may suffer and lose the trust of partners.

> [!QUOTE] The storm analogy
> An organization must prepare for the storm, ensuring it has the tools to **mitigate** and **quickly respond** to outside threats — minimizing risks and potential damages. *Threat goes in → provide a solution.*

Professionals help **mitigate (reduce) the impact** of these threats.
