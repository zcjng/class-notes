# 🎣 Phishing & Social Engineering

[[Google Cybersecurity|← Back to Course Hub]] · [[2026-10-14|Related day: 2026-10-14]]

## 📌 Phishing

**Phishing** is the use of digital communications to trick people into revealing sensitive data or deploying malicious software.

> [!TIP] Defense
> **Conduct regular internal training** so employees recognize phishing attempts.

### Common Types

- **Business Email Compromise (BEC)** — a message that appears to come from a known source to make a seemingly legitimate request for information, for financial gain.
- **Spear phishing** — a malicious email attack targeting a **specific user or group**; appears to come from a trusted source.
- **Whaling** — a form of spear phishing targeting **company executives** to gain access to sensitive data.
- **Vishing** — exploiting **voice communication** to obtain sensitive information or impersonate a known source.
- **Smishing** — using **text messages** to trick users into revealing sensitive information or impersonating a known source.

---

## 🕸️ Social Engineering

- **Watering hole attack** — a threat actor attacks a **website frequently visited by a specific group** of users.
