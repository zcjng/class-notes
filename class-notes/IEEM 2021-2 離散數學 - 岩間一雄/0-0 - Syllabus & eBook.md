## Syllabus

Math logic, algorithms, number theory, proof techniques, graph theory and graph algorithms
As mentioned above, our main goal is not to learn as much stuff in the textbook
as
possible, but to increase the ability of applying basic ideas of disc math for
attacking/solving specific problems the students will encounter in their carrier
lives.
So, we will be going rather quickly for basic notions/definitions in each
chapter, just
focusing on what is minimum needed for the following specific examples/problems.
We plan to go through up to Chapter 11 of the following textbook, but as
mentioned
above we will not exhaust the whole contents but rather selectively. Therefore
the
overall speed of teaching is rather slow.
