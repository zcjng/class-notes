Start of Exercise: with Chapter 5 (approx. in 2-3 weeks)  
  
Some preliminary advice:  
The lecture book is very well organized, self-containing, and has a large number of additional exercise questions. Learning only by the book will work. Whether you come or come not to the lecture will therefore be up to you. You need to identify your way of best learning, some learn better with support by the lecture and others not. Important is, that you reserve regular time for each lecture.  
  
When I was student, I went to lectures mainly to meet friends (but we also had some courses where it was worth going just for the lecture). If I did not go - I still reserved the very same time for home learning. Instead of going to the lecture, I was working on the book at the very same time. This time was very important.  
  
For exercises, please do not use AI - instead meet with friends and discuss how to approach the questions. Nobody will check, if you used AI for the exercise and I do not care. That is your responsibility. However, in the exams, I and you will see the difference.  
  
AI: My biggest advice for all of your studies: Try to live without AI in your studies. AI can give huge benefits - and at the same time, it is your biggest enemy. You need to practice and train your skills - this is true for every discipline. Using AI will (often) give the right result - but you missed a chance to train yourself and you will never have a second chance to compensate for it. University is for you to develop independent skills, which will become your biggest asset in your life. This, you cannot achieve with AI.  
  
all the best,
