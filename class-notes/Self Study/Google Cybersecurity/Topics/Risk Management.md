## 🔄 The NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)

The **NIST RMF** describes steps for managing risk across a system's lifecycle:

1. **Prepare** — set up risk management activities and identify initial controls *before* a breach happens.
2. **Categorize** — analyze how potential impacts to system confidentiality, integrity, and availability affect the organization.
3. **Select** — choose, customize, and document the specific safeguards needed.
4. **Implement** — put the selected security and privacy plans into action (e.g. updating password policies).
5. **Assess** — check whether controls are working correctly and efficiently, to find weaknesses.
6. **Authorize** — take accountability for remaining risks and align security plans with organizational goals.
7. **Monitor** — continuously check systems and make changes as needed.

## 🎲 Common Risk-Response Strategies

| Strategy | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Acceptance** | Accept the risk to avoid disrupting business continuity. |
| **Avoidance** | Create a plan to avoid the risk altogether. |
| **Transference** | Transfer the risk to a third party to manage. |
| **Mitigation** | Lessen the impact of a known risk. |
