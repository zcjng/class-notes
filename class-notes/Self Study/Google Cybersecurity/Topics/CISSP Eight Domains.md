# 📖 CISSP Eight Domains

[[Google Cybersecurity|← Back to Course Hub]] · [[2026-10-14|Related day: 2026-10-14]]

The **CISSP Common Body of Knowledge (CBK)** is organized into **eight distinct domains**.

---

| # | Domain | Covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Security and Risk Management** | Governance, compliance, legal regulations, business continuity, and risk management frameworks (such as the **NIST RMF**). |
| 2 | **Asset Security** | Data classification, privacy protection, and the lifecycles of physical and digital assets. |
| 3 | **Security Architecture and Engineering** | Secure design principles (**Least Privilege**, **Zero Trust**), cryptography, and vulnerability mitigation. |
| 4 | **Communication and Network Security** | Secure network structures, communication channels, and cloud infrastructure protection. |
| 5 | **Identity and Access Management (IAM)** | Controlling physical and logical access to assets — authentication methods and identity provisioning. |
| 6 | **Security Assessment and Testing** | Vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, log analysis, and auditing strategies. |
| 7 | **Security Operations** | Day-to-day concepts — **Incident Response**, disaster recovery, forensic investigations, resource provisioning. |
| 8 | **Software Development Security** | **Application Security**, secure coding practices, and security within the software development lifecycle (SDLC). |

---

## 🧩 Notes

- **Identity and Access Management (IAM)** uses the principle of **least privilege** — see [[OWASP Security Principles]].
- **Security architecture and engineering** includes: threat modeling, least privilege, defense in depth, fail securely, separation of duties, keep it simple, zero trust, trust but verify.
